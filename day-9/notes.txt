🧩 Debugging Challenge

The question was:

"workspaces": [
   "app/*",
   "packages/*"
]

But the folder is:

apps/

Your answer:

because website and admin not added in package.json

❌ Not quite.

The real problem is:

The workspace configuration says:

"app/*"

But the folder is actually:

apps/

Notice:

app     ❌

apps    ✅

One missing s means npm cannot find the workspace.

This is a very common mistake.

Score: 7/10
